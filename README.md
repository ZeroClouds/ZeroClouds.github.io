    <h2>Game Art and its Impact</h2>
    <h3>Why is Game Art so Important?</h3>
    <p>Art in video games is incredible important because it helps in creating a visual for the story being told. It is supposed to be appealing to not just the eye, but to the story itself. The art in a game helps communicate a message without using words, which is why I think it is so important to have and make right when producing work.</p>
    <h3>How does Game Art Communicate?</h3>
    <p>When it comes to making art for games, the visuals in the game assist in telling the story even when there may not be a direct story being told. An example of this could be a fps game with a series of maps that are based off of ruined cities. The art in that situation would depict the story of an abandoned city that has now resulted to war. Another indicator that helps tell a story within art is color. Using certain color palettes can help evoke emotions and give a player a reading on the situation that may be upcoming depending on the colors of a character or scene. For example, red is often associated with either passion or evil, yellow is often associated with warmer feelings like joy and comfort, and blue is associated with calmer or more meloncholy feelings like sadness or relief.</p>
